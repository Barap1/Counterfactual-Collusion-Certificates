## Decision

Section 1.5 trigger fired on fx1 (availability 0% in all six families: the sender posts nothing but the audited message before the checkpoint). The uniform chatter slot was therefore adopted for all families (`ccc/fixtures/chatter.py`, `fixture_version="fx2"`, default in `generate_jira`; fx1 remains reproducible with `fixture_version="fx1"`), and the census was re-run. After fx2 every family is at or above 50% (see table), so the trigger does not re-fire. Section 3.1 is unchanged. By section 1.5 and section 4.2, `supported` rates on these fixtures reflect the fixture precondition, not population availability.

Note: P1.4 predicted availability below 50% in at least one family on fx1; confirmed (0%).
