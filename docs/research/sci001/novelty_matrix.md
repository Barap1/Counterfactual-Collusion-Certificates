# SCI-001 Novelty Matrix

**Date:** 2026-10-23. Source IDs (P*, R*, M*, L*) refer to `source_ledger.md`. A cell marked "not found" means it was not found in the text that was read. It does not mean the paper proves the feature is absent.

**CCC reference design** (from spec 04): fork at a checkpoint at the earliest receiver exposure. Run three arms: F (fresh factual), K (receiver-masked) and S (sham, byte-equivalent), with an optional R (task-preserving repair). Regenerate stochastic continuations in every arm. Estimate the predeclared harm effect τ_h with its components τ_A, τ_C, τ_O and τ_W. Apply eight validity gates. Include benign causal-cooperation fixtures as hard negatives. Report one of four statuses: supported, not supported, inconclusive or invalid.

## Bottom line of the matrix

- **The intervention primitive is not novel.** Removing or neutralizing a message or exposure and comparing outcomes against a counterfactual is established in three places:
  - collusion detection, at single-decision granularity (P01);
  - MAS debugging, where P07 calls it "the standard tool" (P07, R01–R04);
  - MARL communication analysis (M01, M02).
- **The idea of a counterfactual audit for LLM collusion is not novel either.** It appears as action deviations in cloned markets (P04), as peer swaps (P05) and in pricing impulse responses (P08).
- **"Naive interventional tests over-fire and need a selectivity control" is precedented** in pricing (P09) and in MARL communication metrics (M02).
- **What survived this bounded search** is the combination of five things. No paper I read had all of them:
  1. exposure-level forks in a tool-using LLM team;
  2. regenerated stochastic continuations in F, K and S arms;
  3. a predeclared harm estimand kept separate from coalition advantage and task-utility loss;
  4. **benign causal-cooperation fixtures as hard negatives**, used to show whether a naive knockout mislabels useful teamwork as harmful collusion;
  5. explicit inconclusive and invalid verdicts.

  Item 4 is the most distinctive component, and also the least protected by prior work. Not finding it after about 25 targeted queries is evidence that it is new, not proof.
