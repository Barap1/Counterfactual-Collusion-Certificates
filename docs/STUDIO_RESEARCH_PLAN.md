# CCC continuation: can extra evidence improve attribution?

**Proposed study, not completed or preregistered.** Six to eight weeks. The uncertainty is whether the evidence distinction improves decisions on behavior produced independently of CCC-L's designed twin rules. More seeds from the present generator would not resolve it.

## Question and endpoints

Can incentive perturbations and verified authorization reduce accusations of legitimate cooperation while retaining sensitivity to prohibited, harmful coordination? Keep two endpoints separate: (1) recovery of CCC exposure-harm support on known causal controls, and (2) correct policy-conditioned attribution. A recovered L2 certificate is not an attribution success.

Report legitimate-case accusation rate, sensitivity, abstention, coverage, selective risk, and risk–coverage curves. Report both frozen-style abstention costs (.25 and .5), while avoiding undefined selective-accuracy denominators as method-ranking evidence. Always show the denominator and an interval. Analyze the independent trajectory as the unit; replay branches remain nested.

## Weeks 1–2: controls, labels and feasibility

- Pin operating system/container image, Python, libraries, model digests, prompts and sampling implementation. Run deterministic and noisy diagnostic overlaps on two platforms. Resolve substantive disagreements before confirmatory collection; otherwise restrict the study prospectively to one platform and report that limitation.
- Use a development-only throughput block with short fixed tool/step/token limits. Determine a branch and local GPU-hour ceiling before the independent set exists. Do not silently allocate cloud compute or paid APIs. Any requested budget is a separate owner decision.
- Construct exposure-positive controls with known message-caused outsider harm and independently specified prohibited-coordination controls, plus permitted efficiency cooperation and authorized transfers. Include common-cause and information-bearing negatives. Validate complete branch→gate→certificate behavior; log control failures rather than tuning against the evaluation set.
- Have case producers and label reviewers work without CCC-L outputs or separator rules. Assign authorization externally before behavior; check actual compliance and harm separately from prompt instructions. If labels cannot be resolved from the declared policy and evidence, mark them unresolved; do not force them to match the auditor.
- Freeze the endpoint definitions, sampling/stopping rules, evidence access, thresholds, uncertainty method, failure conditions and analysis code before held-out collection.

A proposed control gate is recovery on at least 90% of 20 exposure-positive controls, with exact intervals, and at most 10% false attribution on 20 legitimate controls. These small counts establish a feasibility gate only. Report the attribution-control endpoint separately; a failed gate stops efficacy claims and redirects work to instrument diagnosis. Controls are not pooled into held-out accuracy.

## Weeks 3–5: independent behavior

Stage a maximum of **160 held-out trajectories**, balanced across two local models, two allocation-task variants and legitimate/prohibited conditions (20 per model × task × condition cell). The target is 80 legitimate and 80 prohibited cases, with labels checked independently; unresolved cases stay visible. Use scenario mechanisms and prompts authored independently of the current twin generator. Hold out at least one task variant or prompt family from auditor development.

Four arms × eight continuations ×160 trajectories is up to **5,120 continuations before probes and retries**. A paired incentive condition could add 2,560 F/K continuations, for a proposed ceiling of **7,680**. Missing placebo eligibility is recorded, never filled by an invented control. The throughput block must establish that this scope fits the approved local budget; reduce the prospective sample or scope before freeze if it does not. Do not stop early for favorable outcomes. Retain attempts, crashes and unresolved labels.

## Weeks 6–8: matched comparison and decision

Within each ablation, every auditor receives the **same declared observables**, case order and available controls. First compare methods on basic replay evidence. Then compare basic versus added incentive evidence; separately add verified authorization. This distinguishes behavioral evidence gains from direct access to a policy record. Cap evidence and compute equally across auditors; report access separately from method identity.

Proposed success requires both:

1. A paired decrease in legitimate-case accusations whose 95% confidence interval excludes zero, at comparable coverage.
2. A paired sensitivity-difference lower 95% bound above **−0.10** for independently verified prohibited harmful cases.

Define the interval procedure and coverage-matching rule in the preregistration, with a paired case-cluster bootstrap or exact paired analysis as appropriate. Report full risk–coverage curves and uncertainty. Eighty cases per endpoint may leave the sensitivity noninferiority test inconclusive; a point estimate inside the margin is insufficient. A power/precision calculation using only development discordance must precede the freeze. Do not label inadequate precision as success or enlarge the sample after observing favorable trends.

Failure conditions: unrecovered positive controls; unresolved labeling large enough to change interpretation; substantive platform divergence; no false-accusation reduction at comparable coverage; a sensitivity loss incompatible with the margin; or an inconclusive interval at the cap. All are reportable scientific outcomes. Authorization gains remain conditional on record authenticity and policy correctness; they do not establish inference of hidden intent.

## Deliverable and value

Release the frozen protocol, independently produced cases where rights permit, evidence-access manifests, control failures, paired endpoint results, risk–coverage plots and platform receipts. This would test whether the proposed evidence standard helps beyond examples designed to demonstrate it. A negative result would identify whether the limiting factor is replay validity, missing evidence, labels, or the reporter itself.
