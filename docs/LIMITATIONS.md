# Limitations and release status

## Scientific scope

- The benchmark is scripted. Policy labels, twins and designed separators come from its generator. It does not independently validate collusion labels or detect intent.
- Message removal can remove useful task information as well as coordination. A valid causal influence estimate is not sufficient evidence of collusion.
- The revised attribution rule satisfies full utility in only 2/84 deterministic evaluation settings; the previous version satisfies 5/84. Neither result establishes general accuracy, superiority or false-accusation control.
- Noisy-condition outputs disagree substantively across platforms. The restriction of established findings to deterministic evaluation is post hoc. Both strata and failed prospective predictions remain preserved.
- The real-agent study has eight independent matched-world pairs. Its directional hypothesis was not supported; this does not prove absence of an effect. Prompt conditions are not verified realized-collusion labels.
- There were no supported exploratory real-agent certificates. End-to-end positive-control certificate recovery and useful detection sensitivity remain unestablished. EXP-003 was not completed.

Exact results and correction sources: [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md). Proposed further research: [continuation plan](STUDIO_RESEARCH_PLAN.md), which is a proposal, not a conducted study.

## Reproduction limits

The standard-library headline check works from public files without network or inference. It reproduces recorded-output arithmetic, not agent generation or the bootstrap interval. The complete analysis/test environment depends on an excluded pinned starter, additional packages and local-only pilot traces. Only 9/27 pilot manifest files are tracked. Fresh-install end-to-end regeneration has not been demonstrated. [Commands and requirements](REPRODUCING.md).
