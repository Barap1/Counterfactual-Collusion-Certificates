# Contrast: non-top-5 submissions with null/negative framing (public judge reviews)
Accessed 2026-10-24. "Not top 5" means: not among the five placed projects shown for the sprint (place not stated on the project page). Reviews are paraphrased from the project pages (fetched via a summarising scraper).

## A. "Every Signal We Found Was an Artifact: A Calibrated Control Battery for Secret-Loyalty Auditing" (Alex Kwon; Secret Loyalties, Jul 2026)
Page: https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/every-signal-we-found-was-an-artifact-a-calibrated-control-battery-for-secretloyalty-auditing-gt3s ; PDF: https://pys8vc40ox3z7qxp.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/files/0262ab9676ddc95d-5f520491-adf3-4c69-8765-1d424745a60f-control--batteryv1-.pdf (PDF not opened by me).
Abstract shape: all four detections dissolved under matched controls; "Our null is calibrated: the same battery recovers loyalties we installed at 1.00"; three of own claims retracted; one cryptographic finding survives.
Judge praise: "Table 10 is a deliverable the field should adopt"; layered retractions show honesty; "matches the rubric for ambitious scope executed rigorously"; shows sensitivity (calibrated null).
Judge criticism: dense, "barely hold it together at 32 pages"; "about twice as long as needed, contains conclusions that get taken back later (requiring unlearning)"; tested detectors seen as naive (keyword/text similarity) so less update; need second base family, wider principal search.
Lesson: an honest, calibrated null with positive-control recovery is rewarded on Execution but a 32-page structure that retracts claims mid-paper is punished on Presentation. Put the retractions in one table and state final conclusions once.

## B. "Six Detectors Missed It, Then We Asked: Identifying the Principal ... and the Measured Null That Explains the Failure" (Jainam Shah; Secret Loyalties)
Page: https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/six-detectors-missed-it-then-we-asked-identifying-the-principal-of-two-secretloyalty-organisms-and-the-measured-null-that-explains-the-failure-2u1z ; PDF: https://pys8vc40ox3z7qxp.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/files/5b16f0979dd7a57d-98bfb6b6-1521-43f0-9eb6-0efcf42c7424-REPORT.pdf (not opened).
Abstract shape: ~250+ words, numbers-heavy, pre-registered falsification test, "we report that rather than relax a threshold fixed in advance", simulated threshold z=3.50 vs conventional (17.1% false-positive rate).
Judge praise: solid reusable stats; "highly rigorous, well-written evaluation with clear communication of findings and limitations".
Judge criticism: "No validated instrument confirms the [headline] claim, and the abstract relies on numbers the paper advises not to trust"; limited generalisation across model families.
Lesson: do not headline in the abstract a number the body disclaims; abstracts must match the evidence standard stated in the body (directly relevant to a confirmatory-null paper).
