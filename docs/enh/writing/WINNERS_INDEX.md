## Judging criteria / rubrics (official)

Collusion sprint (our sprint), https://apartresearch.com/sprints/ai-collusion-research-sprint-2026-10-23-to-2026-10-25 (full extract in NOTES_judging_raw.md): three dimensions, scored 1-5 each (same rubric for all tracks): (1) Impact Potential & Innovation ("is this actually new to the field, or replicating recent work?"); (2) Execution Quality ("Competent" 3 = limitations acknowledged; "Strong" 4 = convincing validation, results clearly support conclusions; "Exceptional" 5 = surprising findings, novel methods or unusually robust validation); (3) Presentation & Clarity (2 = "key information buried... or diluted by excessive length"; 3 = problem, method, findings, limitations clearly present; 5 = "a pleasure to read"). Judges may decline to score reports "too unclear to follow". Same three-dimension rubric is shown on the AI Manipulation Hackathon page (https://apartresearch.com/sprints/ai-manipulation-hackathon-2026-01-09-to-2026-01-11) and in project-page review forms (e.g. Omission Attacks page above), i.e. stable across 2026 sprints.
Prizes: $1,000 / $500 / $300 / $100 / $100; fellowship fast-track.
