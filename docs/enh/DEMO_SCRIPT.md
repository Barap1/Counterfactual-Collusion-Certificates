Target duration: **4:30**. Open `demo/explorer/index.html` directly in a browser. This is an offline walkthrough of stored evidence; clicking controls reveals evidence already in the pack. Keep provenance badges visible. Timestamps are recording cues, not experimental measurements.

| Time | On-screen action | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Start at **Start here**, `#/start`. Point to the separate causal, harm, attribution and uncertainty questions. | “A knockout asks what a message did. It cannot alone establish whether the agents colluded. A message can change an allocation and disadvantage an outsider during legitimate cooperation. CCC separates influence, harmful exposure and attribution, and asks what evidence would justify moving between them.” |
| 0:30–1:05 | Click **Open Twin worlds**, then **P2** and **I2**. Scroll through the twin columns to **Verdicts at I2**. Point to the identical-view banner and view hashes. | “These are scripted twins: colluding allocation and efficiency-motivated cooperation. The private labels are evaluator-only; the auditor does not receive them. At this information set, the transcript, factual and knockout outcomes, sham and placebo controls are byte-identical. An auditor restricted to identical evidence must give identical outputs. That is a construction, not a discovery that a detector is accurate.” |
| 1:05–1:40 | Click **I4**. Show the added environment probe, continuation actions and **Verdicts at I4**. Point to which methods actually differ. | “Now we lower outsider costs on the contested task. The probe supplies separating evidence for this designed policy pair. Different views make different outputs possible; the table shows which methods use the difference. This is the central new-prospective exhibit: an executable evidence boundary and its separating frontier. It applies within this generator and declared policy space.” |
| 1:40–2:05 | Select **P3**; click **I4**, then **I5**. Show the authorization record and verdict table. | “A different ambiguity needs different evidence. For unauthorized versus authorized transfer, the frontier is the external authorization record. Behaviour alone does not supply that record. This assumes a trustworthy record and the declared norm; it is not a universal ethical definition of collusion.” |
| 2:05–2:50 | Click **Study**, `#/study`. Pause on the primary estimate, interval and **NOT SUPPORTED** decision; then show the world-level strip plot. | “Here is the real-agent study, EXP-002. Original-confirmatory: Delta equals 1.354 reward units, world-cluster 95 percent confidence interval minus 0.020 to 2.834, exact one-sided sign-flip p equals 0.0703, across eight matched world pairs. Both required criteria fail. The preregistered directional hypothesis is NOT supported. This is not evidence that the effect is absent. The contrast compares prompt regimes, not realized collusion. The independent observations are matched world pairs.” |
| 2:50–3:20 | Scroll to **Certificates** and **Scheduled continuation records**, then expand **Sources and sha256**. | “Descriptive certificate accounting: zero supported, eleven not supported and five inconclusive. | 3:20–3:50 | Open **H02_B1** from the case list (`#/case/H02_B1`). End-to-end positive-control certificate recovery and sensitivity remain unestablished.” |
| 3:50–4:30 | Return to **Twin worlds**, select **P2**, then **I2**. Keep the identical-evidence banner visible while stating the benchmark status. | “The prospective status is explicit: RQ1 and RQ2 pass by construction; RQ3, RQ4, RQ4b and RQ5 failed; RQ6 is descriptive. The final review also flags the cross-platform execution-gate deviation; reporting reproduction is narrower than platform equivalence. EXP-003 was not completed, so we claim no real-agent twin-probe result. The contribution is an evidence contract and inspectable counterexamples, with negative validation results retained. Every attribution claim should name its information set and the evidence still missing.” |
