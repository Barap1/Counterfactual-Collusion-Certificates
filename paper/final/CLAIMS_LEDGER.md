## Novelty and literature boundary

The defensible contribution is an executable, inspectable evidence contract and counterexample family for the gap between message effects and normative attribution, with an abstention-aware evaluation and explicit failed criteria. It is not the invention of replay, matched controls, three-way verdicts, cooperation/collusion distinctions, or indistinguishability. The information frontier is relative to a finite, specified policy space; the benchmark supplies its oracle labels and designed separators. RQ1/RQ2 and selected RQ5 successes establish construction consistency. A closed-world Bayes reference is not a real-world guarantee.

Citation identities and claim scope are checked against X, not newly re-fetched in this task. Appropriate compact bibliography: Nakamura et al.
